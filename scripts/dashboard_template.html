Global Lead Intelligence

Open evidence on lead contamination in consumer products

Prototype: six public sources harmonised into one schema — 15,159 measurements, 197 country profiles, 904 recalls and alerts. Data generated on .

Read before interpreting: this data describes the contamination of products, not human exposure — which depends on how much is consumed, how often, and by which route. The sources are not comparable as they stand: 41% of measurements come from investigations opened after a poisoning, so from products already under suspicion (see the Sampling filter below). Pure Earth uses field XRF, less precise than a laboratory. Reference thresholds are indicative (taken from Pure Earth) and are not legal limits.
